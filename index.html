Giving Back · 2016–Present

Art with

Since 2016, Ariel has raised funds for the Michael J. Fox Foundation for Parkinson's Research and continues that commitment today. His ongoing fundraising and community work includes founding Elon Pancakes for Parkinson's and co-creating BTTF Project 85, a fan-driven tribute remake of Back to the Future organized as a philanthropic campaign supporting the Michael J. Fox Foundation for Parkinson's Research.
